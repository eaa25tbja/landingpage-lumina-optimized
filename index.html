Product information

This speaker combines Scandinavian elegance with rich, powerful audio, the LUMINA One is designed for a lifestyle where music brings people together. Featuring smooth, rounded corners, an elegant soft-touch handle for effortless carrying, and a compact profile standing at 30 cm high and 20 cm wide, it is lightweight and beautifully crafted.

It's the ultimate companion for every shared moment.

Key elements
  • Immersive Sound: Up to 100 dB of clear, dynamic audio for any outdoor hangout.
  • Nordic Design & Lighting: Curved edges, fabric mesh, and customizable LED modes (Chill, Party, Sunset). Available in Beige, Silver, Black, and Brown.
  • Portable & Compact: 30 x 20 cm, 4.5 kg, with a built-in handle and matching tote bag.
  • All-Day Battery: Up to 18 hours of playback with fast USB-C charging.
  • Seamless Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3, AUX, and multi-speaker pairing (up to 4 units).
  • Social Connect: Share and blend playlists with friends via the app.
  • Weather-Resistant: IPX5 rating against rain and splashes.
